# MD&A: buckets, typical sub-headings, facts to look for

| Bucket (section index) | Sub-headings reports use | Facts worth quoting |
|---|---|---|
| Industry view | Economic overview; Global / Indian economy; Industry structure and developments; Housing finance industry; Opportunities and threats | Industry growth and size figures **with the source the report names**; regulatory changes in the year; government housing schemes mentioned |
| Business and product mix | Company overview; Business overview; Product portfolio; Operational performance; Segment-wise or product-wise performance | AUM and loan book at year end; disbursements; mix by product (home loans, LAP, construction finance, others); salaried vs self-employed; average ticket size; average LTV; average tenure; on-book vs off-book (assigned, co-lent) AUM |
| Distribution | Distribution network; Geographic presence; Branch network; Digital initiatives; Sourcing channels | Branch count and states; branches added or closed; sourcing mix (direct, DSA, connectors, digital); employees |
| Asset quality commentary | Asset quality; Credit quality; Collections; Provisioning | Gross and net Stage 3 (NPA) ratios; provision coverage; collection efficiency; write-offs; management overlay. **Leave out restructured-book details.** |
| Funding and liquidity | Borrowing profile; Resource mobilisation; Liquidity; Asset-liability management; Treasury | Borrowing mix by instrument and lender type; cost of borrowings; incremental cost; funds raised in the year; NHB refinance; credit ratings; liquidity buffer; liquidity coverage ratio where stated; ALM position |
| Risk management | Risks and concerns; Risk management framework; Internal control systems and their adequacy | Named risks and the mitigation stated; changes to the framework; internal audit set-up |
| Outlook | Outlook; Way forward; Strategy; Future plans | Every target or commitment, in management's words, with its horizon |

Other headings often present, kept under their own names: "Discussion on financial performance with respect to
operational performance" (extract the table and the explanations), "Key financial ratios" / "Details of significant
changes in key financial ratios" (extract in full), "Human resources" (employee count), "Cautionary statement" (note
its presence only).

## Quoting versus summarising: the test

Ask of each sentence: could an analyst put this in a model or hold management to it next year? If yes, quote it with
its page. If it describes, reassures or explains in general terms, it belongs in the two-or-three-line summary.

Do not join two sentences into one quote. Do not trim a quote so that a qualifier ("excluding", "on a standalone
basis", "before exceptional items") is lost.
