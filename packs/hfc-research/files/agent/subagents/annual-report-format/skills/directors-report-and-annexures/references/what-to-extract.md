# Board's Report: sub-headings and what the analyst needs

Headings vary; match by meaning. If a report uses a different name, use the report's name in the extract.

| Sub-heading (typical wording) | Extract |
|---|---|
| Financial summary / Financial highlights / Financial results | The table: total income, finance cost or total expenses, PBT, tax, PAT, OCI, appropriations. Both years. Unit from the table header. |
| State of the company's affairs / Operational highlights / Business performance | Sanctions, disbursements, AUM, loan book, number of branches and employees, as facts quoted with pages. (The KPI subagent uses quarterly sources for its own tables; here they are annual context.) |
| Dividend | Per-share amount, face value, interim/final, total outgo if stated, dividend distribution policy link |
| Transfer to reserves | Statutory reserve u/s 29C NHB Act; special reserve u/s 36(1)(viii) Income-tax Act; general reserve; debenture redemption reserve if any |
| Share capital / Capital raised | Changes in authorised and paid-up capital; preferential issue, rights issue, QIP, ESOP allotments; amounts and dates |
| Resource mobilisation / Borrowings / Debt | NCDs (public issue, private placement), commercial paper, bank term loans, NHB refinance, ECB, public deposits (accepted, unclaimed, matured unpaid), securitisation and direct assignment in the year |
| Credit rating | Agency, instrument, rating, outlook, changes during the year |
| Capital adequacy | CRAR with Tier I and Tier II, against the regulatory minimum the report states |
| Asset quality / NPA | Gross and net NPA (Stage 3) amounts and ratios as the report states them. Leave out restructured-book details. |
| Directors and Key Managerial Personnel | Appointments, re-appointments, cessations, retirements by rotation; independent directors' declarations; KMP changes |
| Meetings of the Board / Committees | Number of meetings; defer details to the Corporate Governance Report |
| Subsidiaries, associates, joint ventures | Names, changes; "none" statement; pointer to AOC-1 |
| Deposits | For deposit-taking HFCs: deposits accepted, outstanding, unclaimed; compliance statement |
| Particulars of loans, guarantees, investments (s.186) | Usually a statement that the section does not apply to an HFC's lending; quote it |
| Related-party transactions | The policy sentence and pointer to AOC-2 and the notes |
| Auditors | Statutory auditors, their tenure, any qualification and the Board's reply; secretarial auditor; fraud reporting by auditors u/s 143(12) |
| Risk management / Internal financial controls | One or two quoted sentences; defer detail to MD&A |
| Material changes after the balance-sheet date / Significant orders by regulators | Quote in full: these are facts analysts look for |
| Annexure list | Which annexure letter is which |

## Annexures

| Annexure | What it is | Extract |
|---|---|---|
| Form AOC-1 | Salient features of subsidiaries', associates' and JVs' financial statements | Entity names, holding %, and the table. Often placed after the consolidated statements instead. |
| Form AOC-2 | Contracts or arrangements with related parties u/s 188 | See the `related-parties-and-aoc2` skill. A "Nil" / "not applicable" form is a finding. |
| Secretarial audit report (Form MR-3) | Practising company secretary's report on compliance | Auditor's name, period, every observation or qualification quoted in full; or the clean sentence. Also the secretarial compliance report if annexed. |
| Annual report on CSR activities | CSR policy, committee, obligation and spend | Average net profit, 2% obligation, amount spent, amount unspent and where transferred, ongoing projects. Amounts to crore. |
| Particulars of employees (s.197(12), Rule 5) | Remuneration ratios | Ratio of each director's remuneration to the median; % increase for directors and KMP; % increase in median remuneration; number of permanent employees. The top-ten-employees statement is often not printed: say so. |
| Conservation of energy, technology absorption, foreign exchange | Rule 8(3) particulars | For an HFC usually a short statement; extract foreign exchange earnings and outgo (relevant for ECB) |
| Dividend distribution policy, nomination and remuneration policy | Policies | Presence and link only, unless asked |

## Quoting and summarising

- Quote: numbers, dates, names, regulator actions, commitments ("the Company will..."), auditor's or secretarial
  auditor's observations, anything said about defaults, frauds, penalties.
- Summarise in a line or two: descriptions of policies, committee charters, compliance boilerplate, acknowledgements.
- Never characterise ("strong growth", "worrying rise"). Report what is printed.
