# Headings as reports print them

The machine-readable table is `section-headings.json` (same folder). This page says the same in words, and what to
do when a heading is not in the table. Trust the document in front of you: if a report names a section differently,
record the heading as printed (`title_as_printed`), map it by hand, and report the new wording.

| Section | Headings commonly printed | Not to be confused with |
|---|---|---|
| Corporate information | Corporate Information; Company Information; General Information; Corporate Directory | "Corporate Overview" (a marketing chapter) |
| Board's / Directors' Report | Board's Report; Directors' Report; Report of the Board of Directors; Board's Report and its Annexures | "Annexure ... to the Board's Report" (an annexure, not the start) |
| Management Discussion and Analysis | Management Discussion and Analysis; Management's Discussion & Analysis; MD&A. Sometimes an annexure to the Board's Report rather than its own chapter | A one-line reference to it inside the Board's Report |
| Corporate Governance Report | Report on Corporate Governance; Corporate Governance Report | Auditors' / Practising Company Secretary's Certificate on Corporate Governance |
| BRSR | Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report; Business Responsibility & Sustainability Reporting; BRSR | The assurance statement on BRSR |
| Standalone financial statements | Standalone Financial Statements; Standalone Financials; just "Financial Statements" when the company has no subsidiaries | |
| Consolidated financial statements | Consolidated Financial Statements; Consolidated Financials | Form AOC-1 (salient features of subsidiaries) |
| Independent Auditor's Report | Independent Auditor's Report; Independent Auditors' Report; "... on the Standalone / Consolidated Financial Statements" | Secretarial Audit Report; Annexure A / B to the auditor's report (CARO, internal financial controls) |
| Notice of AGM | Notice; Notice of the 28th Annual General Meeting; AGM Notice. Often a separate document for listed companies | |

## Note-level headings (inside the statements)

Notes are numbered and the numbers differ in every report. The search looks for a line that starts with a note
number and carries one of these:

| Section | Heading wording |
|---|---|
| Loans | "7. Loans"; "Note 7: Loans (at amortised cost)" |
| Borrowings | "13. Debt securities"; "14. Borrowings (other than debt securities)"; "15. Deposits"; "16. Subordinated liabilities" |
| Ind AS 109 | "Financial risk management"; "Credit risk"; "Credit quality of assets"; "Impairment assessment"; "Reconciliation / analysis of changes in gross carrying amount and ECL allowance"; stage-wise tables also sit inside the Loans note itself |
| Transfer of loan exposures | "Disclosure pursuant to ... Transfer of Loan Exposures"; "Details of loans transferred / acquired through assignment"; "Securitisation"; "Transferred financial assets" |
| RBI HFC Directions disclosures | "Disclosures required by / pursuant to / in terms of the Master Direction ... Housing Finance Company (Reserve Bank) Directions"; older reports: "... Housing Finance Companies (NHB) Directions"; sub-headings "Capital to Risk Assets Ratio", "Maturity pattern of certain items of assets and liabilities", "Exposure to real estate sector", "Principal business criteria" |
| Related parties | "Related party transactions"; "Related party disclosures"; "Disclosure in respect of related parties pursuant to Ind AS 24" |
