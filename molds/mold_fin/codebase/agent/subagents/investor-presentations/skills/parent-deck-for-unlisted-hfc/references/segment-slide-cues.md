# Finding a housing-finance subsidiary inside a parent's deck

## Where it sits

| Parent deck section | What it gives for the subsidiary |
|---|---|
| Group overview / "business at a glance" | One tile: AUM, maybe PAT |
| Consolidated AUM break-up by product | A "Home loans" / "Housing" line. **Product, not entity**: check |
| Segment divider + 3 to 8 slides titled with the subsidiary's name or "Housing finance" | The best source: AUM, disbursements, mix, asset quality, sometimes branches |
| "Subsidiary performance" / "Key subsidiaries" table near the end | One column per subsidiary: AUM, net worth, PAT, GNPA |
| Appendix: entity-wise financials | P&L and balance-sheet summaries per entity |

## Title and text cues

`housing finance`, `home finance`, `home loans`, `mortgages`, `affordable housing`, the subsidiary's legal
name and its short brand name, `HFC`, `subsidiary`, `segment`, `business-wise`, `entity-wise`.

## What is usually available, and what is not

| Metric | In a parent deck? |
|---|---|
| AUM / loan book of the subsidiary | Usually |
| Disbursements | Often |
| Product mix, ticket size, LTV | Sometimes |
| GNPA / NNPA | Usually (not yours to file) |
| Branches of the subsidiary | Sometimes; often only a group or shared-branch figure |
| Employees of the subsidiary | Rarely |
| Sell down / buy out | Rarely at entity level |
| The subsidiary's own yield, cost of funds, spread, NIM | Sometimes; often only group-level |
| Definitions specific to the subsidiary | Rarely; the group glossary applies |
| Guidance specific to the subsidiary on the parent's call | A few sentences, if any |

"Usually" and "rarely" describe decks in general. The deck in front of you decides.

## Entity or product?

Use the figure as the subsidiary's only when at least one holds:

1. The slide title or a label names the legal entity.
2. The deck states that the product is carried entirely in the subsidiary.
3. The figure sits in an entity-wise table.

Otherwise it is a group product line. Report it as such and write no row.

## Worked example

Parent deck slide 12 "Consolidated AUM ₹ 80,000 crore: Gold 30%, Home loans 28%, Business loans 20%, ..."
and slide 30 "Example Home Finance Ltd: AUM ₹ 5,000 crore". 28% of 80,000 is 22,400, far more than the
entity's 5,000: the parent books home loans itself. Only slide 30 is the subsidiary. Never compute the
subsidiary's AUM from the product share.
