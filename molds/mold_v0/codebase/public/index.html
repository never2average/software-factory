Why APO, why now

Developers stopped picking tools. Their agents do it now.

A developer types “add background jobs” into Claude Code, Codex or Cursor and accepts the diff. The agent chose the queue, installed the package and wrote the config. Nobody compared vendors. Nobody read your landing page.

  • The decision is invisible

    It happens inside a private session on someone else's laptop. No click, no referrer, no signup funnel to measure.

What is APO

Agentic Preference Optimization, defined.

APO is the practice of measuring and improving how often AI coding agents choose your product while they do real work. It is to coding agents what SEO is to search engines: you can't buy the decision, but you can earn it, and you can't improve what you don't measure.

The APO loop, run by Octillion

  1. Profile your product

    Give a one-paragraph pitch, your site and your docs. Octillion researches them and builds the profile: names, real package names, competitors and the developer personas you sell to.

    name: YourDB · pkgs: yourdb, @yourdb/client · vs: OtherDB

  2. Pose blind problems

    Realistic tasks your buyers hand to agents, like set up auth for this FastAPI service, that never name you or a competitor. The agent has to arrive at you on its own.

    scenario × starter repo × persona

  3. Run the real agent mix

    Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, OpenCode and more work each task in isolated sandboxes, in proportion to how developers actually use them, until the code is really done. A simulated developer answers their questions in persona.

    agent mix ∝ developer usage · full transcripts

  4. Score, fix, re-run

    Every run gets a verdict with transcript evidence. See where you lose and why, improve your docs, examples, SDK or packaging, then re-run the same tasks to prove preference moved.

    verdict → evidence → fix → re-measure

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is APO?

Agentic Preference Optimization is the practice of measuring and improving how often AI coding agents choose your product while they do real work: installing your package, importing your SDK, or recommending you to the developer. Octillion is the platform for it.

How is APO different from SEO or GEO?

SEO optimizes for people reading search results. GEO optimizes for chatbots answering questions. APO optimizes for agents making decisions mid-task, in a real repo with real constraints, where the outcome is code rather than a link or a mention. A brand that chatbots mention can still lose when an agent actually writes the code, so APO measures what agents do, not what they say.

Does Octillion change what the models do?

No. Octillion never pays for placement, injects prompts or touches the models. Agents get blind tasks that don't name you. You improve the things agents actually learn from and act on, such as your docs, quickstarts, examples, SDK ergonomics, package metadata and error messages, and Octillion measures whether those changes moved agent preference.

Which models and agents do you test?

Octillion runs scenarios across multiple frontier coding models, so you can compare how each one behaves on the same task. The exact model lineup evolves as new models ship; tell us which ones matter to your users and we'll cover them during early access.

What counts as a “recommendation”?

We separate it into verdicts. Adopted means the agent actually installed, imported or configured your product. Recommended means it explicitly advised using you. Mentioned is a passing reference. Competitor means it chose one of the alternatives you configured. Your recommendation rate counts Adopted and Recommended, and every verdict links to the transcript lines behind it.

Are the agents told about my product?

No. Agents are blind to who is asking. They get a realistic task and a real repo, the same way a developer would hand them work. Your product context is used only to score the transcript afterwards, never to prompt the agent.

How are transcripts stored?

Each run's raw transcript is recorded and kept with its verdict so you can audit any result. Transcripts are scoped to your organization's account. Ask us during onboarding if you have specific retention or data-handling requirements.

Can I bring my own scenarios and repos?

Yes. Scenarios should mirror what your buyers really ask agents to do. We'll help you shape a starting set during onboarding.
