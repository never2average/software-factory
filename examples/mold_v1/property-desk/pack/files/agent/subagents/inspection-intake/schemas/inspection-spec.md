# Inspection intake: the desk's rulebook

The maintenance desk's rules for turning an inspection report into tickets, kept as the coordinators gave them.
`instructions.md` restates them; where the two disagree, this file wins.

## Sources

1. An inspection report is the inspector's: never change what it says was found. Read it, name the place, rate it.
2. The building's open tickets (the calendar's maintenance tickets for that building) win over the report on whether
   something is already being fixed: a defect an open ticket covers is reported as a duplicate, never filed twice.

## Places

3. Flats are "Flat <number><letter>", upper case, whatever the report calls them (apartment, apt, unit).
4. Floors are L<n>: ground is L0, the first basement B1.
5. Common areas use these names only: Stair <letter>, Lift <n>, Roof, Plant room, Bin store, Car park, Lobby,
   Corridor L<n>.
6. A place the building does not have, or one that cannot be named, is never guessed: the defect is listed with the
   reason and not filed.

## Severity and due dates (from the inspection date, calendar days)

7. **Urgent, 1 day (R1):** gas, sparking or exposed wiring, no heat or hot water, water coming in, a ceiling down, a
   fire door that does not close or latch, a fire or smoke alarm fault, someone trapped in a lift, a broken ground-floor
   window.
8. **High, 3 days (R2):** leaks, damp, mould, handrails, trip hazards, a lift out of service, door entry and intercom,
   emergency lighting, security doors and gates, broken locks.
9. **Normal, 14 days (R3):** cracks, loose fittings, stains, paint, sealant, grout, blocked or slow drains, lights out.
10. **Low, 56 days, the next quarterly round (R4):** cosmetic marks, scuffs, graffiti, weeds, signage.
11. The first rule that matches decides. Nothing matched: Normal, and say so ("default").

## Raising tickets

12. Tickets are filed only when the coordinator asks, after validation, one per defect, under the building, due on
    the rulebook's date, with the report line and the image (for a photo log) in the ticket's notes.
13. Urgent defects are also named first in the reply, so a person can phone the contractor.

## What to leave out

14. Items the report marks as passed or not applicable.
15. Anything about the residents beyond the flat number.

## Open points

- **Several buildings in one report.** Not decided; meanwhile one building per run, and other buildings' items are
  listed as not read.
- **Repeat defects.** Whether a defect found again after its ticket was closed reopens the old ticket or files a new
  one is not decided; meanwhile a new ticket, with "seen before: <old ticket>" in its notes.
